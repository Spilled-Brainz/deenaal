People can only meet you as far as they’ve met themselves.

Frustration stems when there is a mismatch between expectations and reality. Sometimes it’s hard for us to percieve how someone cannot see or do something that’s painfully visible. Yet, you must consider, what is visible for you. Could not be visible towards others.

Reality is, especially if you’re both naturally talented and skilled. Meaning, you inherently have it and you’ve worked on it. Example: perception and a certain form of literacy. There will be gap in you with you and others. That’s normal and to be expected.

Whenever dealing with someone and you feel frustration come through. I want you to question, whether you’re measuring them with your baseline or theirs. A reminder that people can only meet you as far as they’ve met themselves.
