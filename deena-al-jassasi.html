Deena Al Jassasi is an Emirati strategist, entrepreneur and psychology-trained advisor based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, working across human behaviour, strategy, technology and relationship intelligence.

The through-line

Why people do what they do.

Across psychology, corporate strategy, analytics, entrepreneurship and private advisory, Deena's work has been built around one recurring question: why do people behave the way they do, and what changes when you understand the system underneath the behaviour?

Her work sits where human psychology meets real-world consequence: decisions, power, identity, relationships, leadership, business, technology and the environments that shape people. She is particularly interested in moments where the personal and professional overlap and where conventional advice stops being useful.

That perspective became the foundation of her Mental Programming Method and later informed her work in mental-health technology, human performance, private strategic consulting and relationship intelligence.

Experience

Across people, data and strategy.

Corporate

Analytics & strategy

More than a decade of experience within Emirates Group, spanning analytics, commercial thinking, planning and strategic decision-making. The work developed a strong bias toward evidence: understanding patterns, interrogating assumptions and separating signal from noise before acting.

Psychology

Academic and research foundation

Deena studied Psychology at Concordia University, alongside Law & Society and Religion. Her academic background included research exposure in PTSD within the Douglas/McGill ecosystem, adding scientific discipline to a long-standing interest in trauma, cognition, identity and behaviour.

Entrepreneurship

Health technology & human performance

She founded Brainz Health, a mental-health technology venture, and later worked across human performance and healthspan. These experiences deepened her interest in how technology can support people without flattening human complexity.

Advisory

Psychological strategic consulting

Today, Deena works privately on complex cases where psychology, strategy, people, identity and consequential decisions overlap. Her work draws on behavioural insight, analytics, strategic reasoning and pattern recognition rather than a single disciplinary lens.

Current ventures

Maktoub & Lumiora

Her current work extends into relationship intelligence through Maktoub, a private matchmaking and social concept, and Lumiora, a developing relationship-intelligence system designed to examine readiness, relational patterns, risk and compatibility more deeply.

Education & formation

Psychology first. Then range.

Deena's formal education began in psychology, but her working range expanded deliberately. Law and society added institutional thinking. Religion added another lens on meaning, culture and collective behaviour. Analytics trained her to quantify patterns. Entrepreneurship exposed those ideas to consequence.

Her professional life has also crossed diplomacy, media, corporate environments, scientific research, health technology and private consulting. Rather than treating those as separate careers, she uses them as different vantage points on the same problem: how humans think, decide, relate and build systems around themselves.

Writing

Ideas before categories.

Deena writes about psychology, strategy, technology, relationships, power, ethics and the systems that shape human behaviour. Her essays often begin with an observation that does not fit neatly inside one discipline, then pull from several to explain it.

Her writing includes work on intelligence and moral responsibility, founder limitations, emotional intelligence, feminism, AI and mental health, cumulative consistency and the psychological structures beneath everyday decisions.
