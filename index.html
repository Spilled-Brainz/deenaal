Deena Al Jassasi created the Mental Programming Method to combine her unique insights, experience and academic background in psychology, strategy and technology. From a young age she observed individuals and society and asked a single question: why do we do what we do. Is it individual thought? Collective pressure? Religious shaping?

That curiosity was forged with a love for books. At fourteen, Al Jassasi encountered Robert Greene's work on psychological warfare. Her world was never the same. She formalised this interest academically; a degree in Psychology, a minor in Law and Society, and a focus on Feminist Islamic Religion from Concordia University.

After work spanning diplomacy, scientific research and media, she built her own practice; combining her frameworks on psychology, strategy and the technology shaping human behaviour. Initially called The Guide to Hacking Your Psyche, later formalised as the Mental Programming Method. Built on one question: do you run the programme inside your head, or does the programme run you?

She built her practice in Montreal, then Riyadh and Dubai; where she is from. She speaks English, Arabic and French.

Your voice says more than the human ear can hear.
